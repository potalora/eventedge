# Supported filing evidence and operational acceptance

The approved goal extends PR #50 with native-text PDF evidence, verified ownership history, citation and attribution repairs, and a prospective supported-document policy. It explicitly broadens the earlier exact-ten material exception. The goal does not authorize a merge, production mutation, deployment, paid API fallback, or increased resource budgets.

Every discovered required filing must have complete acquisition and a reproducible outcome. Supported analyses must satisfy their existing requirements. A deferred record must carry independent source evidence for an allowed reason and must never create orders. Model statements alone cannot establish an exclusion. Retrieval failures, truncated evidence, parser defects, invalid model responses and exhausted budgets remain failures.

The allowed deferred reasons are material visual content that native text cannot represent, genuinely ambiguous ownership lineage after bounded verified acquisition, and independently established unresolved publisher facts. OCR/vision, speculative fact correction, strategy/model/risk changes and new source families remain out of scope. Existing exact quarantines remain valid under their original contracts.

Pedro subsequently authorized verified government copies on October 11: "Allow verified government copies and keep going." This is a narrow exception to the source-family restriction for acquiring the same already-discovered regulatory notice. The opt-in `regulation_retrieval_policy="verified_government_copy_v1"` supplements `regulation_evidence_policy="complete_document_text_v1"`. A GovInfo Federal Register rendition must match the recorded notice number, publication date, agency, proposed-rule section, docket and page inventory, contain the complete notice, and retain all original discovery and attachment obligations. It must not expand discovery or replace a missing attachment. Preserve the original failed download response and the alternate original bytes with their separate identities, hashes and observations. Reconstruct identity, extraction and any supported transport transformation from those originals during immutable replay. Unknown markup, visual placeholders, parsing failures, truncation and exhausted budgets remain failures. All requests and bytes share the existing source deadline and caps; the fallback never starts a new budget. This authorization does not include deployment, production changes or paid access.

PDF handling must preserve original submission and decoded PDF hashes, every document/page, text provenance and quality findings. Complete text does not prove complete visual meaning. The parser must conservatively identify unsupported visual/layout content and must fail on malformed, encrypted, missing or truncated extraction. Existing submission, selected-document, parser-process, corpus, spool and deadline bounds remain binding.

Pedro subsequently approved a separate structural visual proof on October 11: "Allow the explicitly limited structural proof." The new opt-in `verified_supported_documents_v2` may defer a selected PDF after complete acquisition and strict structural verification of the entire file, all pages, content operations, referenced resources and invoked image framing. It must explicitly leave JPEG entropy/pixel validity, native Unicode/glyph interpretation, text visibility and visual meaning unverified. This is a narrower proof claim than the native-text contract, not a successful native extraction. The receipt grants no supported analysis or order authority, and original full-analysis coverage remains false. Strict acquisition, PDF graph, content and encoded-stream framing defects, missing resources, unknown structural capabilities and exhausted budgets remain failures. V2 must be selected prospectively before parsing; a caught native-parser exception cannot authorize deferral. Immutable replay independently reconstructs the same structural receipt from the retained original. V1 and all historical failures remain unchanged. Actual lossless decoding and inventories retain their existing aggregate bounds; no JPEG pixel decoder or OCR is added, and declared hypothetical raster size is reported separately from actual decoded work.

Ownership history is separate from annual comparison. Preserve subject and filer/reporting-person identities, all securities, relevant base and intervening amendments, explicit incorporated agreements and chronology. Submission metadata alone, a previous-accession field, or one reporter CIK cannot certify a chain. Ambiguity and unavailable evidence must be distinguished.

Keep strict source identity and contiguous exact quotation validation. Any bounded corrective model attempt retains the original failed attempt and fits the same model deadline. Never accept malformed output by normalization that changes substantive claims.
