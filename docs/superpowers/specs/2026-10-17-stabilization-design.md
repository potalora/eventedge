# Sixteen-book stabilization design

Approved scope: retain all sixteen books, defer unproven document features, and complete the reliability changes identified in the October 17 review. This is a prospective behavior change requiring a new generation after separate deployment authorization.

## Operating scope

Retain the existing four horizons (`30d`, `3m`, `6m`, `1y`) and four size profiles (`5k`, `10k`, `50k`, `100k`). Keep the SQLite accounting ledger, protective execution, governed prices, risk gates, immutable observations, prospective decision clock and reviewed model transports.

Use one checked-in, secret-free stabilization profile. Bulk filing research remains `disabled_v1`; explicitly classify its three dependent strategies (`insider_activity`, `filing_analysis`, `quantum_readiness`) as deferred while preserving SEC issuer identity checks used by focused litigation. Retire `regulations` and its `regulatory_pipeline` strategy through the existing explicit source lifecycle contract. Disable bounded document research and decision shadow. Do not fall back to thinner regulatory evidence. Keep existing unrelated declared exclusions, including congressional display-only scope and the unsupported state-event proxy.

Every exclusion must be bound to the frozen profile and represented in the complete strategy-health roster. An exclusion is neither a successful extraction nor evidence of full research coverage. The supported strategies must still produce healthy research outcomes; all-held research, model failures and missing required evidence cannot count as supported completion.

## Completion contract

Use one predicate for supported-profile completion across operational reporting, recovery/controller acceptance and release continuity. Require exact generation/code/profile identity, sixteen valid accounting results, sixteen completed staging results, complete durable evidence, complete health classification and successful work for the admitted strategy scope. Reject unknown exclusions, duplicate/missing books or health rows, contradictory receipts, required-source failure and accepted-state corruption.

Preserve the existing distinction between operational completion and full research coverage. Keep historical clean/degraded outcomes inspectable. Five actual consecutive sessions on the frozen configuration remain a separate continuity qualification; constructed integration tests do not establish native reliability or research performance.

## Source capabilities

Opt the stabilization profile into `source_capability_policy=regime_strategy_capabilities_v1`. Historical profiles retain existing projection behavior.

Persist complete acquisition outcomes before allowing partial-provider reuse. Independently validate FRED's shared HY spread, yield curve and VIX requirements; an optional payroll-series failure must not discard accepted shared inputs. Keep conservative commodity and state dependency compartments. Preserve original errors, partial data and hashes. Validate aliases, observation bounds and finite current values without substituting an older observation for a failed current one.

For Yahoo, separate shared VIX from the research-price basket. Keep the research basket atomic in this release: its existing truncated failure diagnostics cannot prove per-symbol success, and survivor sampling would change strategy semantics. Failed frames must not enter an accepted price cache. Missing/unscoped acquisition proof remains unavailable; malformed or contradictory accepted evidence fails closed. Reconstruct the same projection and receipts from immutable inputs at replay and admission.

## Durable restart

Seal the accepted shared economic input bundle, epoch, code/profile identity, exact cohort roster, per-book scopes and starting-state commitments before the first book mutates economic state. Reuse the existing immutable source store; introduce no separate database or daemon.

On recovery, validate the whole seal and every book before any resumed effect. Existing bound contexts remain authoritative. Bind unstarted books only to the sealed input and unchanged starting state. Close the existing gap between overdue-intent cancellation and initial context binding using the ledger's transaction facility. Preserve phase-chain, volatility, borrow, corporate-action and later-session checks. A missing seal in a historical interrupted session does not authorize synthesis.

Recovery performs no provider/model calls and creates no late historical decision. Missing committee work remains a missed sample. Repeated recovery must not duplicate fills, cash events, snapshots or staging.

## Worker ownership

Use the existing process-group owner for ordinary daily workers as well as preflight workers. Preserve full versioned worker output needed for result validation; do not truncate it into a diagnostic tail. Timeout, termination and leader exit must reap descendants and release inherited locks. Add no watchdog, scheduler or generic retry framework.

## Finite acceptance

Run focused regressions during implementation, then the existing two full non-live CI jobs on the frozen change. The exact-profile composed gate must exercise the real controller, shell, manager, worker and sixteen ledgers with external IO replaced by deterministic fixtures.

Required cases: two sessions with positive paper intent/fill lifecycle; zero-external-call replay; the known NaN/null regression; optional and required source failure; corrupt accepted evidence; first/middle/last book interruption at distinct durable boundaries; overdue cancellation before context binding; and hung/abandoned descendants. Every case has an explicit complete, held/missed or operator-required result and checks for duplicate financial effects.

Keep document unit/replay regressions. Separate the deferred full graphical corpus qualification from the stabilization gate; no expansion of that corpus or native model campaign is part of this release.
