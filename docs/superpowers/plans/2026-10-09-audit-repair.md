## Portfolio and evidence decisions

1. **Causal daily-bar clock.** Prior holdings' open gap stops and previously decided next-open exits settle first. Admit next-open entries using only cash/exposure and net realized losses available at that open. Then settle non-gap intraday stops, with conservative session-close timestamps (daily bars do not identify actual trigger time). An intraday exit cannot finance an earlier open entry. No intraday entry/re-entry simulation. New open entries may be exposed to same-day protective stops only where an actual persisted stop exists; do not invent intraday trajectories.
2. **Risk accounting.** Value all open quantities at exact session open after verified corporate actions for admission. Daily loss means negative net realized session P&L after fees/slippage and known cash charges, against pre-entry opening equity, derived durably at admission rather than mutable counters. Winning exits offset losing exits. Existing configured percentage unchanged. Fees/slippage enter prospective cash/equity/exposure checks. Configured stop cooldown derives from durable exit reasons and exchange session dates. Disabled optional gates must not be advertised as active; enabled gates with missing required evidence fail closed.
3. **Cost/margin model.** ACT/365 borrow and financing use actual calendar days carried since the preceding accepted session; Friday-to-Monday is three days, with prior carried notional for the closed interval and explicit handling of positions opened/closed today. Persist charge identity for restart equality. Maintain a conservative marked collateral reserve using the configured short initial-margin factor against current liability, and block additional exposure on a deficiency; do not claim to simulate broker liquidation/locates. Report this as a paper execution model.
4. **Corporate actions.** Missing/NaN action evidence is unavailable, not zero. Same-session terms use post-split per-share dividend basis, with splits applied before dividends independent of record IDs. Reject ambiguous terms. Preserve past marks; use coherent current marks. Untraded signal outcomes need action coverage too. Signal evaluation uses next-open to Nth-session-close total shareholder return (splits and cash distributions), direction-signed, explicitly gross of hypothetical trade costs; net executable returns come from ledgers.
5. **Evidence requirements.** A content-dependent thesis requires source text and successful validated analysis. Missing content/model output produces a non-actionable observation with a reason. Deterministic insider/COT/weather rules may remain actionable only under an explicit complete-input contract; optional model failure cannot fabricate evidence or erase a valid deterministic thesis. Retire unsupported occurrence-only bullish filing/WARN and momentum substitutes as event strategies. Preserve distinct event identities until aggregation; late observed events may become eligible exactly once later.
6. **Source clocks and coverage.** Keep observation period, known publication time, and acquisition time distinct. FRED historical requests bind a real-time vintage; sources lacking trustworthy historical publication/vintage support must refuse fresh historical reconstruction rather than guess release Fridays or silently use latest data. Frozen accepted bundles remain usable. Current COT availability is conservatively acquisition time unless actual release evidence exists; fetch enough annual history for requested lookback. Use exhaustive event windows under the existing budget for EDGAR EFTS/USAspending; explicit bounded samples for per-issuer Form 4, CourtListener, Regulations and Congress retain sampling/truncation metadata. Missing requested environmental observations are unavailable, not neutral. Complete cutoff-late candidates retain their original thesis in the ledger and enter the first eligible session's normal candidate validation before staging; moving source windows and reacquisition cannot strand them or backdate changed facts.
7. **Research claims.** SPY and BIL daily returns must be computed from adjacent observations acquired in one adjustment vintage, with immutable pair evidence. All mature scoped signal outcomes participate in accuracy. Event studies are descriptive total-return catalyst studies, not executable alpha or independent-event significance: preserve timing/anchor metadata, require contiguous exchange sessions, and avoid IID significance claims on overlapping events. Governed next-open outcomes and net portfolio ledgers are the forward-test authority. Clef remains non-trading shadow; report coverage and distinguish evidence support from return prediction.
