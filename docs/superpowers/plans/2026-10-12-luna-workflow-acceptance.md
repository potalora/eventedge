# Bounded Luna workflow and simulated operational acceptance

This finite engineering acceptance supersedes the five-live-day prerequisite.
The persistent local continuity experiment remains optional, paused, and is not
an engineering acceptance gate. No production deployment or active generation
patch is authorized.

## Implementation boundary

- Add opt-in `bounded_document_research_v1`: at most four explicitly selected,
  hash-pinned retained originals, with exact acquisition timestamps, page/render
  identities and finite extraction questions. No discovery or bulk acquisition.
- Feed those inputs through the ordinary source envelope as a separate
  `bounded_documents` retained-document capability. Existing complete-filing research remains
  disabled in the reduced profile. Registry intake completeness must never mean
  whole-document extraction completeness.
- Call native Luna at the bounded analyzer boundary; validate strict typed
  values, units, rows/columns, page coverage and literal citations before scoped
  Sol analysis. Malformed or uncertain recognition cannot reach Sol. Bind
  attachment identities into request receipts and bypass text-only reuse.
- Structural validation cannot independently prove visual transcription accuracy.
  Preserve that limitation, original failures and unassessed pages. These research
  observations remain journal-only and cannot authorize orders.
- Use ordinary immutable source/screening checkpoints, committee selection,
  16-book staging/accounting and replay. Other valid candidates and protective
  exits continue independently.

Freeze hashes and the historical sequence before executing their respective
acceptance runs. Reuse the existing harnesses and retained inference receipts.

Use no more than two new native Luna calls in total. The first scanned-image
attempt returned correct numbers with missing units and failed independent
acceptance. Preserve that failure, correct the generic unit contract, and use
the remaining call on the same scan with unchanged independent gold. This is a
seen corrective sample; reuse the prior accepted PDF control without another
native call. Replay retained outputs for multi-session tests. Real/replayed
inference and constructed catalysts, clocks, benchmarks and unavailable history
must be labeled explicitly. This establishes operational behavior, not alpha or
a leakage-free historical model backtest.

## Closure

Run focused new/related regressions once after integration, then required CI on
the final topic-branch commit. Fix actual failures and rerun only affected checks.
Publish scenario/expected/observed results and material limits. No calendar wait,
exhaustive source permutations, repeated broad suites, merge or deployment.
