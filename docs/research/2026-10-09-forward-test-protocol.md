# Forward test after the assumption audit

This protocol is fixed before the next generation starts. Its purpose is to learn whether EventEdge produces economically meaningful excess returns under reproducible information and execution rules. The repair itself is not evidence of alpha. Deployment and its starting date remain unapproved.

## Decision and scope

The primary portfolio is `horizon_3m_size_100k`. The three other $100k horizons are dependent sensitivity scenarios; the twelve smaller books test concentration and minimum-ticket effects. Do not average their AUM or count them as independent replications. Do not select the best book after seeing results.

The decision is whether this portfolio improves on passive ETF alternatives. SPY (S&P 500) is the primary opportunity-cost benchmark; VTI (US total market) and VT (global equities) are fixed secondary comparisons. BIL and the exposure-matched SPY/BIL blend explain cash and market exposure. Compare total returns over identical accepted dates, with dividends included. Do not choose the benchmark after seeing which one the portfolio beats. A matched rule-only portfolio is outside this experiment's scope; these results cannot isolate the LLM's individual contribution.

Model roles are fixed before launch: `gpt-6-luna` at `high` reasoning handles structured insider-buy, commodity-macro and agricultural-weather assessments. `gpt-6-astra` at `high` handles unstructured thesis analysis (filings, earnings, compensation, regulation, litigation, supply chains, quantum readiness and insider-sale/10b5-1 assessment) and portfolio synthesis. Validation, eligibility, accounting, sizing and risk limits stay deterministic. This is a workload-based design choice, not evidence that Astra predicts returns better. Role/effort settings enter the generation's semantic identity. Retain configured and returned model identity, response ID and an explicit revision when supplied; a returned alias alone remains labeled unpinned. The published model pages currently provide bare aliases, so provider implementation stability cannot be assumed from our configuration freeze. [Luna](https://developers.openai.com/api/docs/models/gpt-6-luna), [Astra](https://developers.openai.com/api/docs/models/gpt-6-astra).

Within one run's frozen source bundle, exactly identical candidate-analysis requests may reuse a response only after the original candidate passes the existing schema and entity checks. Every receiving candidate is validated independently and retains the original model response identity with an explicit reuse marker. Exact prompts, model/effort and effective provider configuration must match. Failures and invalid output are never retained; reuse does not bypass the shared deadline or survive the run. Weather holding periods remain part of the request, and portfolio committees remain separate decisions.

Net returns include trading and carrying costs already specified below. Per the research scope, model, data and hosting expenses are excluded; no operating-cost allocation subsystem or cost gate is required.

Preserve existing sizing, exposure, conviction and stop thresholds except repairs to their documented units or application. Freeze model identifiers, prompts, strategy configuration and source/clock contracts within a generation. Automatic learning and promotion remain disabled. A material change starts a new generation and evaluation window; earlier results stay labeled with their original contracts. Data failures remain visible and are not retrospectively filled with later knowledge.

The state-economics strategy is explicitly retired: national indicators plus sector momentum do not establish a state-level event. Filing occurrence, unrelated company-name matches and contractor momentum no longer substitute for the named catalysts. Deterministic rules require their specified evidence; content-dependent theses require source text and valid analysis. This may reduce trade count. That is preferable to evaluating an unlabeled mixture of hypotheses.

## Economic conventions

- Decisions use only information available by the recorded cutoff and enter at the next exact XNYS open. Prior next-open exits and open-gap stops can release cash first. Intraday high/low stops settle after opening entries and never finance them retroactively. Their close timestamp denotes unknown intraday timing, not an observed execution time.
- A complete candidate first acquired after the close waits in its immutable ledger observation. At the next eligible close, its original facts, direction, score and model analysis enter the normal price, volatility and portfolio checks, even if the source window has changed. Reacquisition never backdates new facts or replaces that queued thesis. Each canonical event receives one timely offer, including when the committee chooses no trade.
- Entry checks include adverse slippage and explicit fees, coherent opening valuation, marked short collateral and durably reconstructed net session losses. Existing limit percentages stay fixed. Calendar-day borrow/financing is charged on prior accepted closing exposure through the next session; new positions begin accrual at the following session.
- Corporate-action absence must be evidenced. Before resuming held inventory, acquire every missing exchange session since the last accepted mark/acquisition and bind the effective-dated action history. Apply splits once, before post-split distributions and current-session stop evaluation. Preserve standing protective stops; expire missed next-open instructions. Missing continuity blocks resumed accounting. Do not reconstruct missed-day fills or erase invalid periods. Carrying charges use prior accepted closing exposure, translated to its original share basis when a split was already applied.
- Dividends create signed receivables/payables at the ex-date. Entitlement affects equity but is unavailable for purchases until a verified payment date; unknown dates remain outstanding. Short payables reserve buying power. Settlement occurs once in the next processed eligible session and retains that actual settlement date. Short collateral remains a conservative paper reserve; the model does not establish broker locates or forced liquidation.
- Payment dates are joined from Alpaca's native cash-dividend record using an unambiguous symbol/ex-date/rate match. This enriches the date and provenance only, never the original cash amount. Unknown-to-known terms are audited and included in execution-state integrity checks. Closed positions retain their outstanding entitlement; missing or conflicting evidence cannot erase or rewrite accepted terms. A date learned at or after a session's open cannot fund that session's simulated opening purchases. Source failures, due bills and unsupported distributions remain outstanding rather than fabricating cash receipt. [Native action contract](https://docs.alpaca.markets/us/reference/corporateactions-1).
- Benchmark returns use adjacent adjusted prices from the same acquisition vintage, retained as immutable evidence and accumulated into a coherent index. Earlier unpaired benchmark histories are not silently repaired or blended into the new results. New default sessions require the configured SPY/BIL/VTI/VT evidence under the existing complete-input acceptance contract.
- Signal outcomes are next-open through Nth-session-close total shareholder returns, direction-signed and gross of hypothetical costs. Only executed portfolio ledgers establish net tradable performance. Directional accuracy and Clef support probabilities are diagnostics, not substitutes for portfolio returns.

EFTS filing searches and USAspending windows are exhaustive within their stated query bounds and acquisition budgets. Form 4 acquisition remains a bounded issuer sample: at most 40 recent filings per requested issuer, with date bounds, known counts, possible archived records and truncation retained in source health. CourtListener, Regulations and congressional feeds also report their bounded scope. None of these samples establishes exhaustive market coverage.

Portfolio-critical dependencies are held assets, due instructions and benchmarks. Diagnostic outcomes acquire entry/maturity prices and continuous post-entry corporate-action evidence independently. Intermediate outcome prices are unnecessary. Freeze the first diagnostic acquisition, including failure, per ticker/session; no later historical fetch may fill its gap. Missing outcome-only evidence invalidates that obligation and degrades diagnostic coverage while healthy books and outcomes continue. A held ticker retains its original governed price result even if a subsequent action request fails. Completed/stored replay uses bound evidence only. Integrity conflicts in immutable evidence still fail closed.

Obligations keep their original signal and epoch identity across accounting gaps. A skipped maturity becomes explicitly invalid when next processed. Reports separate all observations, provisional hypotheses, validated actionable/neutral signals, committee selections and actual executions. Each population shows pending, invalid and missing matured evidence. Prior-epoch obligations remain visible separately from current valid portfolio performance; scenario books are never pooled as independent trials.

A valid empty committee response is a completed abstention. Model errors and unsupported recommendations hold cash and report degraded decision status. Rule-only selection requires explicit configuration. Freeze the complete decision and policy audit before staging so a crash cannot invoke a new model decision. Prompts include every admitted event's identity, evidence and thesis, and all marked holdings with exposure. Deterministic screen and acquisition budgets retain discovered/admitted/excluded identities and reasons, including earnings-news articles and the declared PQC symbol universe.

NOAA summaries use public NCEI Access daily summaries for every selected GHCN inventory station in the ten-state region, in bounded batches under one 90-second deadline. Completeness means usable TMAX/TMIN/PRCP for every state/day in the contiguous lookback, ending no more than seven days before acquisition. Quality-flagged measurements are counted and excluded; failed batches remain failures. State-day means are equally weighted, without claiming acreage weighting or historical station-universe vintages. USDM uses state FIPS codes and the native categorical schema.

USDA coverage follows declared crop/class/state and comparison-season policies. Corn/soy use the existing ten states; winter wheat uses IL/IN/KS/MO/ND/NE/OH/SD, spring excluding durum uses MN/ND/SD, and durum uses ND. Corn/soy compare June 15 through October 15; winter wheat April 15 through June 30 and November 15 through November 30; spring wheat June 15 through August 15. These conservative strategy periods are distinct from the provider's publication calendar. Active scopes require every state at the common latest week within 14 days. Inactive classes remain recorded but cannot imply weekly deterioration. November winter wheat uses the following reporting year with actual fall observation dates.

SEC ownership queries cover modern SCHEDULE and legacy SC labels, including amendments, and deduplicate by accession. Unverified subject issuers remain journal-only. Government-award events require `new_awards_only`, in-window base obligation dates and native award identities. Their amount is cumulative obligations on the new award observed at acquisition, not an incremental modification or announced ceiling. CourtListener opinion searches retain cluster identity and nested opinion IDs separately.

SEC ownership indices can link a subject's index to a reporting filer's document directory under the same accession. Accept only actually listed SEC links bound to that exact accession; the document directory does not establish the subject issuer. Raw/stylesheet aliases coalesce only when the listed raw counterpart shares the document directory, sequence, form and XML filename. Distinct documents remain ambiguous and unavailable.

Award issuer attribution requires reviewed native UEI identities or an award-bound verified parent UEI, followed by the reviewed issuer mapping. Recipient-name substrings cannot authorize a ticker. Current reviewed coverage is deliberately limited to BA/LMT identities recorded in the crosswalk; unknown or conflicting recipients remain journal-only with incomplete attribution coverage. Verified issuers receive admission priority, then the existing score/ticker/stable-identity order applies. Unknown awards remain in the complete discovery manifest and can occupy remaining journal slots, but cannot displace verified issuers solely because of a larger amount. This is a bounded issuer sample, not all US government contractors.

COT observation dates are not publication dates; the CFTC documents delayed publication and a release calendar. Current acquisitions use conservatively known availability, and historical reconstruction without release evidence is refused. FRED historical acquisitions bind a vintage. These choices follow the [CFTC release contract](https://www.cftc.gov/MarketReports/CommitmentsofTraders/ReleaseSchedule/index.htm) and [FRED real-time periods](https://fred.stlouisfed.org/docs/api/fred/realtime_period.html).

## Fixed readouts and decision rule

At 30, 60 and 90 completed exchange sessions, review continuity, missing/invalid periods, source and analysis coverage, trade count, net return, benchmark excess, maximum drawdown, volatility, turnover, carrying costs, concentration, and dependence on single names/catalysts. Review problems and risk breaches immediately; do not change the thesis solely because a short window looks poor or strong. Report actual valid return count: 30 returns normally require 31 valid snapshots.

Treat the following as a **research hurdle**, not a forecast: at least 5 percentage points of annualized net excess versus SPY, positive excess versus the exposure-matched SPY/BIL benchmark, annualized net Sharpe of at least 1, and maximum drawdown no worse than the existing 15% drawdown limit. Meeting these after a short period only justifies continued observation.

Do not make a strong alpha claim before at least 252 valid out-of-sample daily returns. One year is a minimum review point, not proof: disclose uncertainty, market-regime concentration and dependence among trades. Before any capital decision, require uncertainty estimates that respect serial dependence/overlapping events, sensitivity to higher costs and removal of the largest contributor, and evidence that results are not just market/sector exposure. If the sample cannot support those checks, the conclusion is inconclusive. No automatic promotion follows from a threshold pass.

Generation reports compute fixed-execution cost sensitivity at 1x/2x/3x recorded costs, realized-PnL contributor concentration/removal, lagged SPY/BIL exposure attribution, and seeded circular-block return uncertainty at 5/10/20-session block lengths with at least six blocks. Each result declares its evidence scope. Realized-only contributor stress cannot establish a complete portfolio counterfactual; exposure residuals are not causal alpha. Executable-fill validation requires independent timestamped executable quotes, and probability calibration requires held-out probability forecasts tied to matured targets. Both remain explicitly unmeasured when that evidence is absent. Offline fixtures cannot supply those empirical observations.

ETF uncertainty resamples the portfolio and benchmark together at identical date indices. Its statistic is annualized compounded portfolio return minus annualized compounded benchmark return, matching the excess-return hurdle. Report all 5/10/20-session block intervals; do not select the favorable interval. Before 252 valid returns the result is inconclusive. Thereafter, the SPY hurdle is supported only when every interval lies above five percentage points, below the hurdle only when every interval lies below it, and otherwise inconclusive. This remains a descriptive estimate under the observed regime, not a guarantee or causal-alpha test. VTI/VT comparisons remain secondary descriptive evidence and never replace the SPY criterion. Observable event-key, ticker and ticker/session counts disclose repeated evidence; they do not estimate an independent sample size.

Precommit the substantive review at 252 valid returns and a final evidence review at 504 valid returns or two calendar years after generation start, whichever comes first. Unresolved uncertainty at the final review is an inconclusive experiment; do not repeatedly extend the window or retune thresholds until significance appears. Record every invalid/skipped session and all earlier epoch windows alongside the current valid window. A reset does not erase elapsed experiment time or past failures. These research reviews do not automatically promote capital, change models, stop services or deploy code.

Descriptive event studies use a common total-return basis and contiguous exchange sessions. Catalyst-date returns may precede a recorded decision and cannot be called executable alpha. IID t-tests/confidence intervals over overlapping journal events are withheld; use the governed forward outcomes and primary net portfolio for the decision above.

## Release acceptance

Before an authorized launch: component regressions and native provider→strategy→ledger paths must pass, lifecycle/report commands must preserve authoritative state, and the whole non-live suite must pass. Preserve audit evidence and resolve independent review findings. Use a fresh generation/metric epoch; do not patch these behavior changes into the active experiment. Five clean sessions establish operational continuity only. They do not meet the performance hurdle.

Check package, credential, entitlement and model access on the intended host, then run the complete sixteen-book source/model/ledger workload in isolated scratch state. Preserve actual candidate/call counts, source and analysis coverage, phase times, failures and completion margin. Source-only or tiny model checks cannot substitute for this acceptance. FRED's actual HTTP transport and native model calls have killable wall-clock bounds. All horizon analyses and committee calls share 2400 seconds; individual model calls have a 120-second ceiling, with SDK retries disabled and budget-aware explicit backoff. Exhaustion must retain incomplete coverage and hold cash, never silently trade a completed prefix of the sample. The outer worker limit remains 3600 seconds.

The launch scope remains long-only daily-bar paper trading. Enabling shorts additionally requires a bound borrow-availability/rate feed. Mergers, ticker migrations, delisting consideration, due bills and other unsupported corporate events require an explicit reconciliation decision; missing held-symbol continuity fails closed. Simulated next-open fills do not establish spreads, available liquidity, market impact or live executable performance.
