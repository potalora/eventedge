# Forward-Test Protocol (`gen_003`)

This protocol is fixed before `gen_003` starts. Its purpose is to learn whether EventEdge produces economically meaningful excess returns under reproducible information and execution rules.

> **Historical note:** Pre-`gen_003` October 2026 failed-campaign logs (`r3`–`r7`, bulk EDGAR/PDF v2/v4/v5 checkpoints, and `gzip_original_v1` spool diagnostics) are preserved in the [collapsed appendix](#archived-october-2026-campaign-logs) at the end of this document. Bulk filing/media acquisition and retired strategies are excluded from `gen_003`.

## Decision and scope

- **Primary portfolio:** `horizon_3m_size_100k` ($100k, 3-month horizon). The three other $100k horizons (`30d`, `6m`, `1y`) are dependent sensitivity scenarios; the twelve smaller books (`5k`, `10k`, `50k`) test concentration and minimum-ticket effects. Do not average scenario AUM, count the 16 books as independent replications, or select the best book after seeing results.
- **Active strategies (5):** `earnings_call`, `supply_chain`, `govt_contracts`, `weather_ag`, and `commodity_macro` (`ACTIVE_STRATEGIES` in `tradingagents/strategies/config.py`). Retired strategies (`congressional_trades`, `litigation`, `filing_analysis`, `insider_activity`, `quantum_readiness`, `regulatory_pipeline`, `state_economics`) are excluded.
- **Benchmarks:** `SPY` (S&P 500) is the primary opportunity-cost benchmark; `VTI` (US total market) and `VT` (global equities) are fixed secondary comparisons. `BIL` and the exposure-matched `SPY`/`BIL` blend explain cash and market exposure. Compare total returns over identical accepted dates, with dividends included. Do not choose the benchmark after seeing which one the portfolio beats. A matched rule-only portfolio is outside this experiment's scope; results cannot isolate the LLM's individual contribution.
- **Model roles and transport:** Fixed before launch under `GenerationConfig` (`chatgpt_plan` OAuth transport):
  - `gpt-6.2-sol` (`high` effort): thesis analysis and 1-call-per-horizon portfolio committee synthesis (`PortfolioCommittee.synthesize_horizon()`, 4 committee calls/session).
  - `gpt-6-luna` (`high` effort): bounded structured assessments for `commodity_macro` and `weather_ag`.
  - Validation, eligibility, accounting, sizing, and risk limits remain deterministic. Role/effort settings enter the generation's semantic identity (`config_sha256`).
- **Response reuse:** Within one run's frozen source bundle, identical candidate-analysis requests may reuse a response only after the original candidate passes schema and entity checks. Every receiving candidate is validated independently and retains the original response identity with an explicit reuse marker. Failures and invalid output are never reused.
- **Cost scope:** Net returns include trading and carrying costs specified below. Model, data, and hosting expenses are excluded per research scope.
- **Freeze discipline:** Preserve sizing, exposure, conviction, and stop thresholds within a generation. Automatic learning and promotion are disabled. Any material change to models, prompts, strategy configuration, or source/clock contracts starts a new generation and evaluation window; earlier results remain labeled with their original contracts.

## Economic conventions

- **Causal decision clock:** Decisions use only information available by the recorded cutoff and enter at the next exact `XNYS` open (`execution_clock_version = "causal-xnys-open-stops-v2"`). Prior next-open exits and open-gap stops settle first and can release cash. Intraday high/low stops settle after opening entries and never finance them retroactively.
- **T+1 open adverse selection & quantity trimming:** Entry checks include adverse slippage (`10 bps` default), explicit fees, coherent opening valuation, marked short collateral, and durably reconstructed net session losses. When a T+1 open fill price (including slippage) pushes a staged-valid entry above allowed notional and the overnight gap is within `max_open_chase_pct` (`0.05`, i.e. 5%), `ExecutionBridge` deterministically trims `fill_qty = floor(max_allowed_notional / unit_exec_price)` and records `trimmed_at_open=True` alongside original vs. filled quantity. If `fill_qty < 1` or the gap exceeds `max_open_chase_pct`, the entry is rejected.
- **1-committee-per-horizon & continuous volatility sizing:** `PortfolioCommittee.synthesize_horizon()` emits one ranked, size-agnostic recommendation list per horizon (`30d`, `3m`, `6m`, `1y`). `PortfolioPolicy` (`portfolio_policy_v2`) applies deterministic per-tier sizing with continuous inverse-volatility scaling (`max_position_pct * (annualized_volatility_floor / sigma)`, floor `0.15`, 60-session lookback) from position #1—removing legacy position-count cliffs.
- **Carrying charges:** Calendar-day borrow/financing (`act365`) accrues on prior accepted closing exposure through the next session; new positions begin accrual at the following session.
- **Corporate actions & dividends:** Before resuming held inventory after a gap, every missing exchange session must be reconciled and effective-dated corporate actions bound. Splits apply once before post-split distributions and current-session stop evaluation. Standing protective stops survive; missed next-open instructions expire. Dividends create signed receivables/payables at the ex-date and affect equity immediately, but remain unavailable as purchasing cash until a verified payment date. Missing held-symbol continuity fails closed.
- **Benchmarks & signal outcomes:** Benchmark returns use adjacent adjusted closes from the same acquisition vintage (`SPY`/`BIL`/`VTI`/`VT`). Signal outcomes are next-open through Nth-session-close total shareholder returns, direction-signed and gross of hypothetical costs. Only executed portfolio ledgers in `generation.sqlite3` establish net tradable performance.
- **Tier-1 vs. Tier-2 fault isolation:** Tier-1 accounting/execution (`alpaca_sip`, `yfinance_actions_benchmarks`, `SessionExecutor`) fails closed on missing or invalid data. Tier-2 strategy/enrichment failures (`finnhub`, `yfinance_research`, `usaspending`, `noaa`, `usda`, `drought_monitor`, `cftc`, `fred`, `yfinance_profile`, `finra_short_interest`, `ken_french`) trip per-strategy circuit breakers and mark only affected strategies `unavailable`, preserving sibling strategies and Tier-1 portfolio accounting.
- **Strategy-specific source contracts:**
  - `earnings_call`: Prioritized deterministically by EPS surprise magnitude, liquidity/market cap, recency, and symbol hash; valid numeric EPS surprises with zero news are retained explicitly as `numeric_surprise_only`.
  - `govt_contracts`: USASpending `new_awards_only` with cumulative obligations on the new award, attributed strictly via the hash-pinned 12-character UEI crosswalk (`config/govt_contracts_uei_crosswalk.json`). Unresolved rows stay `journal_only`.
  - `weather_ag`: NCEI Access v1 daily summaries (`TMAX`/`TMIN`/`PRCP`) for 22 Corn Belt ASOS stations during April–September (`off_season_skipped` October–March), paired with USDA NASS crop conditions and USDM state categorical drought severity.
  - `commodity_macro`: CFTC COT ZIP archives (`As_of_Date_in_Form_YYYY-MM-DD` validation) and vintage-aware FRED macro series.
- **Committee abstention:** A valid empty committee response is a completed abstention (holds cash). Model errors or invalid responses hold cash and report degraded decision status. Decisions and policy audits are frozen in `generation.sqlite3` before staging.

## Fixed readouts and decision rule

- **Diagnostic milestones (30, 60, and 90 completed `XNYS` sessions):** Review continuity, missing/invalid periods, source and analysis coverage, trade count, net return, benchmark excess, maximum drawdown, volatility, turnover, carrying costs, concentration, and dependence on single names/catalysts. Review problems and risk breaches immediately; do not change the thesis solely because a short window looks poor or strong. Report actual valid return count (30 returns normally require 31 valid snapshots).
- **Research hurdle (not a forecast):** At least 5 percentage points of annualized net excess versus `SPY`, positive excess versus the exposure-matched `SPY`/`BIL` benchmark, annualized net Sharpe of at least `1.0`, and maximum drawdown no worse than the `15%` drawdown limit. Meeting these after a short window only justifies continued observation.
- **Minimum alpha review (252 valid out-of-sample daily returns):** Do not make a strong alpha claim before 252 valid out-of-sample daily returns. Disclose uncertainty, market-regime concentration, and dependence among trades. Require uncertainty estimates that respect serial dependence/overlapping events, sensitivity to higher costs (1x/2x/3x recorded costs), removal of the largest realized-PnL contributor, lagged `SPY`/`BIL` exposure attribution, and seeded circular-block return uncertainty at 5/10/20-session block lengths (at least six blocks).
- **Block-bootstrap decision rule:** Resample the portfolio and benchmark together at identical date indices (`annualized compounded portfolio return - annualized compounded benchmark return`). Report all 5/10/20-session block intervals without cherry-picking. Before 252 valid returns, the result is inconclusive. Thereafter, the `SPY` hurdle is supported only when every interval lies above 5 percentage points, below the hurdle only when every interval lies below it, and otherwise inconclusive.
- **Final experiment horizon (504 valid returns or 2 calendar years):** Precommit the substantive review at 252 valid returns and a final evidence review at 504 valid returns or two calendar years after generation start, whichever comes first. Unresolved uncertainty at the final review is an inconclusive experiment; do not repeatedly extend the window or retune thresholds. A reset does not erase elapsed experiment time or past failures.

## Release acceptance

Before launching `gen_003`:

1. **Suite & contract gates:** Component regressions, Wave 0/1 contracts (`tests/test_wave0_contracts.py` and full non-live suite), and lifecycle/report commands must pass.
2. **Host & credential checks:** Verify package installation, API credentials (Alpaca SIP, Finnhub, FRED, USDA, etc.), and `chatgpt_plan` OAuth credentials (`/opt/data/private/eventedge/credentials/plan-auth.json`) on the target host.
3. **5-session continuity gate:** Verify five consecutive clean `XNYS` sessions via `scripts/check_generation_readiness.py` before treating a generation as operationally steady. Continuity establishes operational health only; it does not establish alpha.
4. **Paper-only scope:** Launch scope remains long-only daily-bar paper trading (`long_only: true` in `config/profiles/gen003-clean.json`). Enabling shorts additionally requires a bound borrow-availability/rate feed. Simulated next-open fills do not establish spreads, available liquidity, market impact, or live executable performance.
