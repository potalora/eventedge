## Direct content and association requirements

Every target printed occurrence appears exactly once in standalone text, a physical matrix anchor cell or a local note-body fragment. Exact within-cell/header/note line breaks, punctuation, symbols, numeric strings and meaningful typography are retained. No second transcript, expanded flat-cell graph, copied header text per data cell, Markdown pipes, synthetic labels or reconstructed printing is permitted.

The compact content retains target status separately from context closure, standalone text, tables, notes, cell continuations, non-table visuals and explicit reading order. Positional references use named table/row/column or local text/visual positions and explicit note/fragment positions. Context quotations declare their exact attachment position; runtime supplies the corresponding original ID/SHA. Baseline note letters are not globally unique identities: distinct tables may reuse a marker for distinct note bodies.

Tables are physical rectangular string/null matrices, including header bands, stubs, blank corners, sections and summaries. Matrix dimensions are derived from its actual arrays. Explicit merged spans distinguish covered slots from ordinary local blanks; printed dash/zero/N/A/dollar signs never become null. Named inclusive bounds may explicitly reach the last physical row/column, avoiding model-computed rectangle heights. Every slot and declared span must be bounded, non-overlapping and faithful to the originals.

Headers identify real printing, axis, explicit applicability scopes and acyclic same-axis parent relationships. Inherited headers remain original-attributed context quotations with visible target association; they do not invent target rows or unnamed leaf labels. Unit scopes identify actual printing and explicit table/text/visual consumers; unitless counts, dates and identifiers cannot acquire a fabricated unit. Scopes and associations are explicit, never inferred from proximity.

Notes retain complete bodies, line breaks, baseline markers, exact visible raised/printed consumer markers, named locations and applicability. V14 notes have `marker`, `marker_image_position`, ordered `body_fragments` with `image_position` and `body`, plus `anchors`, `scopes` and `consumer_sources`. The printed marker belongs to the first fragment original. Fragments retain logical source reading order, which may differ from numeric image-position order when image1 is a suffix. Each `{note:i,fragment:j}` literal or reading-order pointer must identify a local image1 fragment. Every local fragment occurs once in reading order; foreign fragments receive no local coverage. A note with any local fragment can retain explicit foreign consumers; wholly foreign notes require local anchors/scopes and no reverse consumer sources. Marker provenance, complete fragment order and associations are reviewed without inventing a repeated marker on an unmarked suffix. Reverse association is also required: when note-only EP06OC26.002 is the target, its local e/f/g bodies must retain their actual consumers in context EP06OC26.001, without inventing a local table or demoting notes to prose. Printed anchors identify marker locations; applicability scopes must also include quantities explicitly qualified by the note body, even beyond the marker column. Scope boundaries must follow source meaning rather than marker proximity; unresolved applicability fails. Missing or ambiguous consumer/body association fails. The same mechanism supports standalone continued headings.

Split body cells retain local matrix printing/null and ordered before/after context fragments separately. Each foreign fragment requires exact original attribution, visible target anchors and cell/column association. Foreign text is never stitched into a purported one-source quotation, and a local blank does not mean the whole continued logical cell is blank.

All non-table or mixed material remains required: standalone prose/titles/captions; chart axes/ticks/legends/series and particular axis/unit associations; diagram arrows/endpoints; form checkboxes/underlines/blank fields and associated questions/labels; meaningful superscript/subscript relationships. Unprinted axes use direct positional geometry descriptions. Visual estimates remain qualitative/approximate; they do not become exact sampled data. Empty form fields remain empty. Reading order and explicit caption/table/visual relations preserve narrative context without another transcript.

## Independent review, correction and replay

The V14 reviewer sees the entire original target and exact context originals
plus the complete current recognition candidate. It checks every positional
component and relation, all actual printing, blank/span/header/unit/note/cell/
context associations and non-table geometry, together with a whole-original
omissions check. It separately checks that note scopes cover all quantities
qualified by the body, including qualifications beyond the printed marker's
column. Uncertainty or incomplete applicability requires a negative review;
review cannot silently repair recognition or upgrade partial content. V14 `checked_notes` contains an object verdict for marker provenance, ordered associations and applicability, plus an exact boolean array covering every body fragment. Foreign-fragment defects use an affected local consumer or null error location; a foreign fragment cannot be presented as a local pointer.

V13 restored ordinary V11 candidate-visible review and removes V12's extra
literal transcription. This is not a claim of statistical independence or a
source-fidelity guarantee: actual native results still need independent source
comparison. V14 introduces only the generic continued-note representation, with no source-specific answer, row range, typography rule, normalization or additional call. Historical V11 and V13 requests retain their exact bytes and verdicts; the V12 experiment retains its own sealed replay
implementation and original failed result.

The prospective target-level allowance is at most one bounded correction cycle, with at most two recognitions and two reviews. Eligible returned content/schema/structure/literal/coverage/context failures remain retained as failed stages. A corrected recognition must independently pass the unchanged content validator and a fresh image-backed review. A structurally malformed review may be replaced only against the unchanged valid recognition. A well-formed rejecting review may drive a replacement recognition and fresh review. The reviewer never receives prior review verdicts or repair hints; recognition feedback is bounded untrusted data. No third round, hidden transport retry, response normalization or historical failed-output conversion is permitted.

Authentication/billing/model-integrity, source/hash/request drift, invalid encoding, over-limit output, transport/provider failure and deadline exhaustion are terminal. Exact chain eligibility, prior request/raw/verdict/feedback bindings and final acceptance are pure-replayed. Every target has one final successful or failed/unstarted bundle; the two lists must partition the full plan without omissions or duplicates. The downstream thesis call is permitted only after every target and required association fully passes. All completed graphics and the exact prompt-budget proof survive a later thesis/budget failure.

Eight process-global native-stage slots and at most four target workers per document remain. Waiting, construction, validation, replay and correction consume the same inherited absolute 9000-second phase; graphics calls are at most 240 seconds and ordinary Sol/committee calls at most 120 seconds. There is no fresh nested budget. The deadline is a failure gate, not a completion promise: the retained 98-target October 15 corpus needs 196 first-pass stages, up to 392 stages with one correction per target, and failed document work may repeat across horizons. A fresh corpus has its own complete target count. No failed result is memoized as success.

Original limits remain at most seven selected images/eight MiB total, two MiB per original, 256 KiB raw response, 64000 target literal characters and finite 512 table slots/structural objects, 128 notes/context associations and 64 references/list. Each V14 fragment body counts toward literal characters, every fragment contributes one structural object, and each foreign fragment contributes one foreign association in addition to the note identity. Existing maxima are unchanged. Hitting a limit fails honestly; nothing is dropped, downsampled or truncated to pass. Sol receives canonical lossless content plus trusted target/source bindings and reading order, without per-cell expansion or a second transcript. The offline hash-pinned planning tokenizer measures the assembled actual request; unavailable/over-budget input cannot dispatch. Its planning window excludes transport framing and does not establish provider entitlement.
